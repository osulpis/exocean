Vincent LE FOUEST

Professor at La Rochelle Université, France

Coastal modeler

I am Associate Professor at La Rochelle University and part of the LIENSs laboratory since 2013. My expertise relies on ocean-biogeochemical modeling of the coastal ocean, including the land-to-sea interface. I focus on temperate to arctic environments, where I try to understand how changes in land-to-sea fluxes modulate coastal biogeochemistry, plankton and air-sea gas fluxes. To do so, I combine complex numerical ocean-sea ice-biogeochemical models with in situ and space remote sensing observations.
